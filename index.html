How We Keep Prices and Information Trustworthy

✅ Verified Vendor Profiles: Every seller undergoes identity and stall verification before listing products.

🔎 Community Price Audits: On-the-ground spot checks and daily market updates keep prices accurate during pilot testing.

🚨 Crowdsourced Accuracy: Shoppers and vendors can instantly report outdated prices or out-of-stock items.

About PalengkeSmart

PalengkeSmart is a community-centric digital platform designed to connect local shoppers directly with public market vendors through transparent price tracking, stall discovery, and pre-orders.

Our Goal/ Mission

Empowering local market shoppers with daily verified prices while helping small vendors grow their customer reach and minimize unsold inventory.

Community Note: Daily price monitoring is maintained in partnership with local vendors to assist buyers in planning efficient market visits.
